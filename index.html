Not about size

Rome ranks 2nd with 3 rail connections. Buffalo has 10 and ranks 3rd. The bottlenecks are the small towns.

Why these towns

Rome is the Oneida Carry — boats hauled overland between the Mohawk and Wood Creek, the only water route from the Atlantic to the interior other than the St. Lawrence. Little Falls is the gorge. Both were dug through before the Erie Canal existed.

The odds

12 of the 35 towns sit on the canal. Six for six by chance: 1 in 1,757.
